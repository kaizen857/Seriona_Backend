Recommendation
- Recommended safe default remains 4 TagReader slots. The TSAN stress target did not show races at 8 or 16, but this task produced thread-safety evidence on invalid scanner text fixtures, not real-library throughput evidence or successful media metadata extraction. Raising production concurrency needs task-27-style real-library benchmark evidence, which is explicitly out of scope here.
